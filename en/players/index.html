info@data2gain.com
Do I need to understand statistics?+

No. Strokes Gained puts your performance into context: a positive value means strokes gained against your benchmark; a negative value means strokes lost. Interactive charts, tables and the AI agent help you interpret the analysis in a clear, direct way.

How much does Data2Gain cost for coaches?+

For golf coaches and academies the platform is free. You do not need to request demos or pay monthly fees. You can sign up at prod.data2gain.com and start tracking your players’ rounds and game data.

How do I start using Data2Gain as a player?+

Create your account at prod.data2gain.com, log your first round and activate Data2Gain PRO for €9.75/month. You can cancel anytime from your account settings.

Where can I use Data2Gain?+

The native apps for iOS and Android are coming soon. In the meantime, open Data2Gain in your browser on a phone, tablet or computer at prod.data2gain.com.
