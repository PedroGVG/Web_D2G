Do I need to understand statistics?+

No. Strokes Gained puts your performance into context: a positive value means strokes gained against your benchmark; a negative value means strokes lost. Interactive charts, tables and the AI agent help you interpret the analysis in a clear, direct way.

How much does Data2Gain cost for coaches?+

For golf coaches and academies the platform is free. You do not need to request demos or pay monthly fees. You can sign up immediately in the web app and start tracking your players’ game data with zero friction.

How do I start using Data2Gain as a player?+

Create your account at app.data2gain.com, log your first round and activate Data2Gain PRO for €8.95/month. There is no commitment: cancel anytime from your account settings or the app store.

Where can I use Data2Gain?+

You can use Data2Gain via its Web App (accessible from any browser on phone, tablet or desktop at app.data2gain.com) and in native apps for Android and Apple (iOS).
