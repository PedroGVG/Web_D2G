01 · Organisational structure

Your organisation,
as it really is.

Create programmes, categories and follow-up groups, and give each one its lead and assistants. The org chart is built by dragging boxes: easy, visual and with no training needed.

  • Programmes, categories and training camps
  • Automatic groups by age or category
  • Each coach sees their players; management sees everyone

Data2Gain portal screenshot · Sample data

02 · Radar integration

Radar sessions,
inside the portal.

Import sessions from your radar or launch monitor and analyse them next to real on-course play: carry and total dispersion by club, side and top views of ball flight, path vs face and impact on the clubface.

  • Data as measured or normalised
  • Dispersion ellipses by club
  • Every shot one tap from its details

Data2Gain portal screenshot · Sample data

03 · Physical assessment

The body plays
too.

Record a battery of mobility, stability and dissociation tests and instantly spot what is limited or asymmetric. Any limited test becomes a goal in one click, and its progress is tracked.

  • Body map with the areas to work on
  • Tests grouped by block, with illustrations
  • Progress since the previous assessment

Data2Gain portal screenshot · Sample data

04 · Connected goals

From data
to goal.

Set on-course and physical goals for every player and measure progress with their own rounds: strokes lost on 5–10 m putts, percentage of tee shots with the intended flight, or limited physical tests. Every goal shows the starting point, the target and whether the work is reaching the course.

  • On-course goals measured with their rounds
  • Physical goals created from the assessment
  • Start, target and last round at a glance

Data2Gain portal screenshot · Sample data

05 · Rankings & comparisons

Compare what
really matters.

Rank your players by Strokes Gained or any metric and follow them week by week or month by month. Compare players with each other, groups against groups, and the same player in different conditions: round type, course, sample or reference level.

  • Player vs player, group vs group
  • Filters by sample, round type and reference
  • Export the ranking to CSV

Data2Gain portal screenshot · Sample data

06 · Reports

Every report.
One single place.

Generate personalised reports in one click and find them all together: physical assessments, radar sessions and game analysis for every player, sorted by date, area and coach. No more folders, loose PDFs or lost emails.

  • Personalised report in one click
  • By player or for the whole organisation
  • Full history, always available
