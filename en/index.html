info@data2gain.com
Do I need to understand statistics?+

No. Strokes Gained puts your performance into context: a positive value means strokes gained against your benchmark; a negative value means strokes lost. Charts and the AI agent help you interpret the analysis.

How do I start using Data2Gain?+

Open the web app, follow the sign-in process and add your rounds using the options available in the platform. You can review subscription terms before subscribing.

Where can I use the app?+

You can access Data2Gain from your phone, tablet or computer browser at app.data2gain.com.

Do the examples show my results?+

No. The hole demonstration, benchmark comparison and conversation use illustrative data. Screenshots show examples of the product. Your analysis is based on the rounds you add to the app.

Does the player subscription include a coach account?+

The €8.95/month price refers to the Data2Gain PRO player plan. Contact info@data2gain.com for coach, academy, consulting or training terms.
